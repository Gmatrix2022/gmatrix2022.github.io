About

About Me

Kecheng Yang is an associate professor in the Department of Geographic Information Science, School of Earth Sciences, Yunnan University.

He received his B.S. in Earth Sciences from Yunnan University in 1996 and his M.S. in Physical Geography from Yunnan University in 2009, and was promoted to associate professor in 2011. Since 2002, he has taught core courses in the undergraduate GIS program while carrying out research on spatiotemporal data mining, machine-learning-based geospatial data processing, and Web GIS platform technologies.

Research

Research Areas

Spatiotemporal Data Mining

Extracting spatial and temporal patterns, anomalies, and trends from multi-source geographic data to support evidence-based research and decision-making.

Machine Learning for Geospatial Data

Applying machine learning methods to the modeling and processing of geospatial data to improve efficiency and accuracy.

Web GIS Platform Technology

Building practical WebGIS applications and platforms that make geographic information visible, searchable, and interactive in the browser.
